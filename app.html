What Halation won't do

  • No accounting. QuickBooks and Xero stay; we sync nightly and never pretend to be a ledger.
  • No email client. Sends go out from us; conversations stay in your inbox.
  • No social scheduling. The delivery page is the reel; posting it is your call.
  • No stock music, no stock footage, no template marketplace for the work itself. The craft is yours.
